# ADR 0001: Record architecture decisions

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Research software projects make many technical decisions: language, framework, packaging, data model, deployment, testing strategy, and maintenance model. Future maintainers need to understand why those choices were made.

## Decision

We will record significant decisions as Architecture Decision Records in `docs/decisions/`.

## Consequences

- New maintainers can understand the reasoning behind the software.
- Trade-offs are visible.
- Handover becomes easier.
- The team has a lightweight place to capture decisions without turning everything into paperwork. Tiny victory.
